# Online Garage: Frontend Product Requirement Document (PRD) ## 1. Project Overview **Online Garage** is a high-fidelity, trust-centric service marketplace connecting vehicle owners with verified mechanics. The platform focuses on transparency, real-time tracking, and professional-grade precision. ## 2. Design System (Visual Identity) * **Theme:** Light Mode (Primary). * **Color Palette:** * **Surface:** `#f9f9f7` (Clean, off-white background). * **Primary Charcoal:** `#222222` (Headings, primary buttons). * **Safety Red:** `#c00000` (SOS actions, high-priority alerts). * **Success Green:** `#2e7d32` (Verified badges, status indicators). * **Warning Yellow:** `#FFF9E6` background with `#E0C040` borders (Informational banners). * **Typography:** DM Sans (Professional, legible, modern). * **Principles:** Hick's Law (reducing complexity), Miller's Law (chunking information), and the Peak-End Rule (positive resolution). ## 3. Core User Flows & Screen Map ### Flow 1: Onboarding & Identity 1. **Landing Page:** Value proposition (Mechanical, Towing, Spare Parts), trust metrics, and global SOS trigger. 2. **Registration (3-Step):** * Step 1: Personal Info. * Step 2: Vehicle Profile (Make, Model, Year, Plate, VIN). * Step 3: Identity Verification (Government ID upload for "Pay After Service" eligibility). ### Flow 2: Service Booking (The Funnel) 1. **Dashboard:** Central hub showing active incidents, recent bookings, and quick-action cards for services. 2. **Problem Picker:** Categorized selection (Mechanical, Tyre, Towing, Leak, Part Replacement). 3. **Vehicle Selection:** Multi-vehicle selection logic (Main vs. Secondary). 4. **Mechanic Discovery:** Proximity-based results with ratings, specialist badges, and live map view. 5. **Mechanic Profile:** Deep-dive into technician credentials, certifications, reviews, and specialized pricing. 6. **Service Review & Booking:** Final summary of labor, travel fees, and parts before payment. 7. **Payment Gateway:** Two-tier logic based on verification status (Verified = Pay After Service; Unverified = Escrow Pay Now). ### Flow 3: Fulfillment & Tracking 1. **Live Tracking:** Real-time map with WebSocket-driven mechanic coordinates and estimated arrival. 2. **Service Timeline:** Chronological log of diagnostic confirmed, parts prep, and active repair status. 3. **Booking Confirmation:** Post-purchase summary with safety features and tracking share options. ### Flow 4: Spare Parts Marketplace 1. **Parts Catalogue:** VIN-compatible search for Front Brake Pads, Air Filters, Spark Plugs, etc. 2. **Part Detail:** Technical specifications, installation time, and "Genuine" certification. 3. **Parts Checkout:** Scheduling installation with a certified mobile technician. ### Flow 5: SOS & Emergency (High Priority) 1. **SOS Dispatch:** Immediate location detection and emergency service broadcasting. 2. **Medical Dispatch:** Proximity detection for Trauma Centers and "Slide to Dispatch" ambulance confirmation. 3. **Accident Protocol:** Safety checklist, coordinator voice-call integration, and incident documentation. 4. **Mechanical SOS:** Priority dispatch for engine/thermal criticality with remote visual tech inspection. ### Flow 6: Management Suite 1. **Order History:** Comprehensive list of all past service interactions. 2. **Service History Timeline:** A technical audit trail of every repair performed on a vehicle. 3. **Invoices:** Digital billing records with line-item transparency. 4. **Account Settings:** Managing profile, vehicles, and verification status. ## 4. Key Component Library * **TopNavBar:** Global search, SOS trigger, and profile access. * **SideNavBar:** Navigation between Dashboard, Garage, History, and Invoices. * **Action Cards:** Context-aware cards for service selection. * **Status Badges:** "Verified Master Tech," "Reliable," "Fastest Response." * **Map Module:** Interactive Google/Mapbox integration for live tracking. * **Safety Slider:** "Slide to Confirm" interaction pattern for high-stakes actions. ## 5. Technical Requirements for Frontend AI * **Responsive:** Desktop-first (as designed) with mobile-responsive breakpoints. * **Framework:** React with Tailwind CSS for layout. * **Icons:** Material Symbols (rounded) for technical clarity. * **Accessibility:** ARIA labels for status logs and high-contrast text for critical safety information.
