# Online Garage: State Transition & Prototyping Map ## 1. Global Navigation & Core States - **Authentication State:** `Logged Out` (Landing, Registration) | `Logged In` (Dashboard, Booking, Management). - **Verification State:** `Unverified` (Mandatory Escrow, No discounts) | `Verified` (Pay After Service, 10% discount). - **Emergency State:** `Normal` | `SOS Active` (Priority UI, Dispatch tracking). ## 2.
